# Kitrise support

Kitrise is built and supported by digimaps.co.

## Contact

Email **support@digimaps.co** with:

- your store URL (the .myshopify.com address),
- the theme version (Theme settings, Theme information),
- the page and the steps that lead to the issue,
- screenshots or a short screen recording if possible.

Replies are sent on business days, usually within 2 business days.

## What support covers

- Questions about theme settings, sections and blocks.
- Bugs in the theme code.
- Guidance on theme updates.

Support does not cover custom code changes, third-party apps or store setup outside the theme.

## Before you write

Most setup questions are answered in the [documentation](index.html).
